Awarded in the area of Data Science and Statistics by Georgia Tech’s H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering.

Kimberly and J. King Harrison established the fellowship in honor of Robert Goodell Brown, who pioneered the use of exponential smoothing in forecasting.
